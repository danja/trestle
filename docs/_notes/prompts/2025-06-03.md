When the hamburger menu item 'Shortcuts' is clicked, the right panel should open showing only the list of shortcuts. When the 'Console' item is clicked, the right panel should open showing on the console.  

Currently when 'Shortcuts' is clicked, a box appears on the left of screen 'shortcuts-text'. This is no longer required. Instead the right panel should show the list of shortcuts. The right panel shouldn't show tabs, just the name of its current view, either 'Shortcuts' or 'Console'.
